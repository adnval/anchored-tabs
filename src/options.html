Anchored Tabs

Pinned tabs, and folder tabs you choose to anchor, keep an anchor URL. Closing one (Ctrl+W, middle-click, ×) resets it to that URL and hibernates it, and focus moves to a loaded tab instead.

Pinned

    Pin a tab to add it here.

    Folders

    Open the extension's menu on a tab in a folder and choose “Anchor this tab” to add it here.
